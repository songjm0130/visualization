College key performance indicators

URL: https://files.ontario.ca/mcu_2/mcu-caat-kpi-data-2022-23-college-kpis-table-1-en-2024-02-22.csv

You can use this data to compare a college's performance in each category or compare data among colleges for 2021 to 2022 graduates.

Commencing 2000 to 2001, Graduation Rates are based on tracking individual students, where, for example, the 2022 to 2023 KPI Graduation Rate is based on students who started 1-year programs in 2020 to 2021, 2-year programs in 2018 to 2019, 3-year programs in 2016 to 2017 and 4-year programs in 2015 to 2016, and who had graduated by 2021 to 2022. KPI Graduation Rates include changes resulting from the KPI Review and Adjustment process (where required).
